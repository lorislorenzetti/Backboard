How to Show Any Rank in Rocket League on Your Screen

Your real Rocket League rank is tied to matchmaking and your profile — you can't change what others see without actually climbing. But what appears on your screen is a different story. Bakkboard's Fake Rank lets you display any tier, division and MMR per playlist locally, for streams, screenshots or just for fun.

Fake Rank in Bakkboard — set tier, division and MMR per playlist on your display.

What Fake Rank does

Fake Rank changes how rank information appears on your client — the rank badge, division text and MMR number you see in menus and lobby. You configure it per playlist:

  • Tier and division — set any rank from Bronze to Grand Champion and beyond.
  • MMR value — display a specific MMR number alongside the rank.
  • Season rewards — optional season reward level and win count.
  • Per-playlist control — enable Fake Rank for 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3 independently.

Important: Fake Rank is local only. Other players still see your real rank. Matchmaking uses your actual MMR — this does not put you in higher lobbies or affect competitive integrity for anyone else.

Who uses Fake Rank?

Common use cases:

  • Streamers and content creators — consistent rank visuals for thumbnails or scripted content without grinding an alt account.
  • Screenshots and clips — capture lobby or menu shots with a specific rank aesthetic.
  • Private sessions — roleplay or themed nights with friends where everyone picks a rank for the vibe.

How to set it up

  1. Open Bakkboard and go to the Spoofing tab.
  2. Select the Rank section.
  3. Choose a playlist (1v1, 2v2, 3v3) and set tier, division and MMR.
  4. Optionally configure season reward level and win count.
  5. Enable Fake Rank for that playlist — your display updates in Rocket League.

Disable the toggle or reset values to show your real rank again. Each playlist can be configured separately.

Fake Rank vs. real rank progression

Nothing in Bakkboard replaces actually ranking up. Tracker.gg, leaderboards and what opponents see in lobby all reflect your true account stats. Fake Rank is purely a visual override on your machine — it does not change server-side data or what other players see.

If you want to track your real MMR trends over time, Bakkboard's session stats, charts and MMR tracker work alongside Fake Rank and always record your actual match results.

Safe to use?

Bakkboard does not inject into Rocket League or send false data to Psyonix servers. Fake Rank modifies only what your client renders locally. Easy Anti-Cheat does not flag Bakkboard because it operates outside the game process. There is no ban risk from using Fake Rank.
