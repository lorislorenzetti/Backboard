How to Wear Any Title in Rocket League

Rocket League titles are earned through achievements, battle passes and seasonal rewards — you can only wear what you have unlocked. Bakkboard's Fake Title breaks that limit on your screen: swap to any title in the catalog, or create a fully custom one with your own text and colors.

Fake Title — swap any catalog title or customize your own text and colors.

Two ways to use Fake Title

Swap — wear any catalog title

Browse the full Rocket League title catalog inside Bakkboard and pick any title to display — including rare seasonal titles, SSL rewards and limited-event titles you may not have earned. What you see on your screen changes immediately.

Customize — build your own title

Write any custom text and choose your own colors. This goes beyond swapping — you create a title that does not exist in the official catalog, visible on your client only.

In both modes, Fake Title is local only. Other players in lobby still see your real equipped title.

Why use Fake Title?

  • Preview titles — see how a rare title looks on your banner before committing hours to earn it.
  • Stream and content — consistent title styling for overlays and thumbnails.
  • Custom branding — personalized text for clan nights, tournaments or meme sessions.
  • No inventory required — display catalog titles you never unlocked, on your screen only.

How to set it up

  1. Open Bakkboard and go to the Spoofing tab.
  2. Select the Title section.
  3. Choose Swap to pick from the catalog, or Customize to enter custom text and colors.
  4. Enable Fake Title — your display updates in Rocket League menus and lobby.

Disable the feature to return to your real equipped title.

Fake Title vs. unlocking titles normally

Earning a title in Rocket League still requires completing the associated achievement or reward track. Fake Title does not unlock titles in your inventory or show them to other players. It only changes what appears on your client — useful for personal display, not for flexing in lobby.

Pair it with Fake Name for a full custom identity on your screen, or Fake Rank for a complete profile makeover locally.
